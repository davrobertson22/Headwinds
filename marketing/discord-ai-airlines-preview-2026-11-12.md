**Coming to new worlds: AI airlines** 🤖
*Headwinds · in testing, not live yet*

Most routes in most worlds have no competitor. We're building an option for new worlds that fills the map with airlines run by the game.

**What they are**
• Real airlines in your world: the same costs, the same demand and the same actions you have. No special rules and no head start. They're founded in week 1 with the world's starting capital.
• Run by a fixed set of rules, not a chatbot. They open routes that pay, lease more aircraft when the fleet is earning, cut routes that lose money and hand aircraft back. They can go bankrupt.
• **Always tagged.** Every AI airline's name ends in (AI), everywhere a name appears, and players can't use that tag.

**What's fair**
• They can't see who is online, who is away or where anyone ranks. They go where the forecast says there's money, whoever flies there.
• They take gates and delivery slots like any airline, but never a player's seat in the world.
• They're ranked in the standings. **Trophies, the podium, season awards and the hall of fame stay players only.**
• An option per new world, off unless chosen. Worlds already running never change.

**The first version is cautious**
• 8 AI airlines per world, one hub each, leased aircraft, capped at 100.
• No cargo, custom cabins, alliances or stock market yet.
• Alone in test worlds they reach about 40 aircraft in two game years. Expect mid-table company, not a rival for the top.

**Next:** a trial in an event world before any regular world gets the option. Tell us what you'd want from them: tougher, more of them, different personalities?
