# Discord post — Airline restarts (2026-07-31)

One post, ~1,900 chars. All Headwinds worlds. Register matches the Old Metal /
Demand model 2.5 posts: lead with what changed, be honest about what it costs,
close by asking for a read.

---

**Going under is no longer the end of your season**

Until today, bankruptcy in a world was permanent. Miss three loan payments or sit six weeks in the red and that was it — every button stopped working, the game told you the world would carry on without you, and it did. For the rest of the season.

From this update you can found a new airline in the same world. **Three times.** After that the world really does carry on without you.

**What a restart costs.** Everything. The fleet, the network, the cash, the board objectives, your alliance seat, every gate you held — gone, and none of it comes back. You start again at the world's current week on standard starting capital, which in a year-6 world means founding a startup against airlines that have been compounding since week one. That is the point. It's a way back in, not a reset button, and it is deliberately a bad trade compared to not going bankrupt.

Anyone holding stock in the airline you lost is paid out at the usual delisting rate, same as if you'd left the world. Codeshare partners are released.

**If you abandoned an airline**, you can use this too — and it fixes something that was quietly broken: the lobby had been offering you a "join world" form that could never work, because the game still had you on file. That now correctly offers a restart instead.

**Also fixed:** the bankruptcy screen told you to head back to the world lobby while covering the link that would have taken you there. And a codeshare with a carrier that went under kept billing you its weekly fee for the rest of its year-long term, for connections that no longer existed. Both sorted.

Your restarts are per world, not per account — three in each world you're in.

Tell me how the count feels. Three is a guess: enough that a bad first run isn't a season wasted, few enough that it still stings.
