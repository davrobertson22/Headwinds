**Fixed in Headwinds: certifying a jet base after you've built it**

@Kat the Fox — "maintenance hub upgrades dont allow aditional certifications." You were right, and it was worse than it sounded. A base could only ever cover the aircraft families you ticked the day you built it. Upgrading a Line Station to a Maintenance Base bought you a second certification slot with no way to spend it, so paying $25M to bring a second fleet type home did nothing of the sort.

Tailwinds got that fixed on 31 July. Headwinds didn't, and I should have caught it sooner. The two games run the same engine, so fixes usually land in both on the same day — but what was missing here was the button, not the maths, and buttons don't travel. Your engine has been able to certify a base for another family this whole time. Nothing ever asked it to.

As of the next deploy:

**Certify a base whenever you like.** Each base now lists the families you fly but aren't certified for, one button each. Heavy checks for that type come home instead of going to a third party, and the outsourced contract for it is offset.

**The price is on the button before you spend.** Free while the level's allowance lasts — one at a Line Station, two at a Maintenance Base, four at a Heavy MRO. Past that you pay once up front and a bit each week, both shown before you click, and it greys out with the figure if you can't cover it.

**Eight families in one hangar** is the ceiling, and the card shows where you stand against it. The build form lets you tick up to eight from the start too, with the extras in the quoted price instead of dropped when you pay.

Nothing was reset — every base keeps the certifications it already has. If you've had one stuck on a single type, open Maintenance and it's waiting for you.

Sorry it took a fortnight to reach this side. Keep the reports coming.
